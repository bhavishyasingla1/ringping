Ring Ping: Call Reminder

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

In brief: Ring Ping is designed as a local-first reminder utility. It does not require an account and is designed not to operate a developer-owned backend, advertising profile, or analytics database.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Ring Ping: Call Reminder ("Ring Ping", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

Ring Ping lets you create a reminder and receive a call-style reminder at the scheduled time. The App does not require an account or login and is designed not to operate a developer-owned backend, advertising profile, or analytics database.

Our data-minimisation approach: Reminder information is intended to remain on your device unless you explicitly choose a system or third-party action that sends information outside the App, such as adding a reminder to a calendar application.

2. Information We Handle

2.1 Reminder information you enter

When you create a reminder, the App may store the information needed to deliver it, such as:

  • Reminder text or title that you type.
  • The date and time you select.
  • Reminder or call preferences you choose, such as volume or retry behaviour, where those options are available.
  • The local status of a reminder, such as scheduled, completed, missed, or cancelled.

This information is intended to be stored locally on your Android device for the App to perform its reminder functions. We do not intentionally transmit this reminder database to a Ring Ping server.

2.2 Device and system state

The App may receive limited system information from Android that is necessary to perform its functions. Examples can include whether notifications are permitted, whether the App is currently active, and the state of a Ring Ping reminder call, such as ringing, answered, declined, or missed.

Such information is processed for the App's operation and is not intentionally uploaded by Ring Ping to a developer-owned server.

2.3 Phone numbers, contacts, and call history

Ring Ping does not need your contacts, address book, carrier phone number, or ordinary phone call history to create a Ring Ping reminder. The App is designed to use Android's supported calling and telecom mechanisms for its own reminder experience rather than accessing your personal contacts or reading your carrier call log.

2.4 Microphone and camera

Ring Ping does not require microphone or camera access for its core reminder experience. It does not record your voice, camera images, or video.

2.5 Location

Ring Ping does not require or request precise or approximate location access. The App does not use location to create, schedule, or trigger reminders.

2.6 Account information

Ring Ping does not require you to create an account, provide an email address to use the core reminder functionality, or sign in with Google or another identity provider.

3. How Ring Ping Uses Information

Information handled by the App is used only to provide the functions you request, including:

  • Creating, storing, updating, and deleting your reminders.
  • Scheduling the reminder at the selected time.
  • Presenting the Ring Ping call-style reminder.
  • Tracking whether a reminder was answered, declined, missed, completed, or retried.
  • Speaking reminder text using the Android text-to-speech service requested by the App.
  • Preparing an event for a calendar application when you explicitly select a calendar action.

Ring Ping does not use your reminder content to build an advertising profile, sell personal information, or make personalised advertising decisions.

4. Local Storage and Data Retention

4.1 Local storage

Reminder data is designed to remain in the App's private storage on your Android device. The App may use Android's local application storage or database mechanisms to keep scheduled reminders available between launches.

4.2 How long information is kept

Reminder information remains on your device until you delete it, the App removes it as part of its normal functionality, or the App's local data is cleared or the App is uninstalled. The App does not maintain a separate Ring Ping cloud copy of your reminder database.

4.3 Android device backup

Android devices can provide backup and restore features controlled by the Android platform, device manufacturer, or your device settings. If your device or Android configuration backs up App data, that backup is handled by the applicable platform provider under its own policies. Ring Ping does not operate or control that platform backup service.

5. Text-to-Speech

Ring Ping can use Android's text-to-speech functionality to speak a reminder aloud after a reminder call is answered.

Ring Ping requests speech synthesis from the text-to-speech engine configured on your Android device. The exact processing location depends on the selected speech engine and device configuration. Some engines may process speech locally, while others may use network services.

Ring Ping does not control the privacy practices of an external text-to-speech provider. If your configured speech engine sends text to an external service, that processing is governed by the provider's own privacy policy and settings.

6. Calendar and Third-Party App Handoffs

Ring Ping may provide an optional "Add to Google Calendar" or equivalent calendar action. This is initiated by you and uses Android's calendar event insertion interface to hand event details to a calendar application installed on your device.

Ring Ping is designed not to request calendar read or write permissions for this handoff. Instead, the Android calendar application handles the event creation flow.

When you choose this option, information such as the reminder title, date, and time may be passed to the calendar application so that you can review and save the event. Ring Ping does not receive your existing calendar contents through this action.

Once information is handed to a third-party calendar application, that application's own privacy policy and data practices apply to its processing.

8. Information We Do Not Collect Through Ring Ping's Own Services

The current Ring Ping design does not intentionally collect or transmit the following categories to a Ring Ping-operated server:

  • Your name or profile information.
  • Your email address for the purpose of using the App.
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Your contacts or address book.
  • Your carrier call history.
  • Microphone recordings.
  • Camera images or videos.
  • Advertising identifiers for personalised advertising.
  • Analytics profiles tied to your identity.
  • A cloud-hosted copy of your reminder database.

This section describes Ring Ping's own current handling. Third-party Android platform components that you choose to use, such as a calendar provider or text-to-speech engine, may have separate data practices.

9. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Ring Ping does not sell your personal information and does not operate a data-broker or advertising business based on reminder content.

Limited information may leave the App only when it is necessary to complete an action you explicitly request or when Android itself provides the underlying platform service. The main examples are:

  • Calendar application: Event details you choose to add can be handed to the calendar app through Android's calendar insertion flow.
  • Text-to-speech provider: The configured Android speech engine may process reminder text according to its own implementation and privacy policy.
  • Android platform services: The operating system may process information as necessary to provide notifications, telecom, alarms, audio, backups, security, and other platform functions.

Ring Ping does not intentionally send your reminder database to these services as a background analytics or advertising feed.

10. Security

Ring Ping uses Android's application sandbox and private application storage for local reminder information. The App does not maintain a Ring Ping cloud database containing your reminders.

No method of storing information on a device or transmitting information through a mobile operating system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your Android device protected with an appropriate screen lock, account security, and operating-system updates.

11. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

Ring Ping is designed without advertising SDKs and without an in-app analytics service operated by the developer. The App does not intentionally create a user profile for advertising or cross-app tracking.

If a future version introduces advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, cloud synchronisation, or another third-party SDK that changes what information leaves the device, this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures will be reviewed and updated as appropriate.

12. Your Choices and Deletion

Because Ring Ping is designed without a user account and without a Ring Ping cloud database, there is no Ring Ping account that you need to close.

You can manage your reminder information directly in the App. You can also clear the App's local storage or uninstall Ring Ping using Android's standard controls. Clearing app data or uninstalling the App can remove locally stored reminders and settings, subject to Android system behaviour and any platform backup or restore process.

You can also control Android permissions and special access through your device's system settings, subject to Android's controls and the requirements of the App.

13. Children's Privacy

Ring Ping is a general-purpose reminder utility and is not designed to create user accounts or intentionally collect personal information from children.

Because the App does not require an account and is designed for local reminder storage, it does not intentionally request a child's name, contact details, location, or other personal profile information for use of the core feature.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us directly in connection with the App, please contact us using the address below so that we can review the request.
